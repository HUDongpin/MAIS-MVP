# Content QA Checklist

## Identity and Scope

- Exact candidate/package ID, version, immutable digest, track, grade, language, item types, and intended surface are bound.
- Reviewer authority, independence, timestamp, reviewed rows/assets, exclusions, and evidence identities are explicit.
- Any mutation after review creates a new identity or a verifiable bounded revision with fresh review.

## Correctness and Pedagogy

- Mathematical premises and requested result are coherent and solvable.
- Accepted answers, equivalent forms, units, precision, domains, and option uniqueness are correct.
- Explanations, worked examples, hints, misconceptions, and difficulty fit the target learner.
- Ambiguity, hidden assumptions, invalid distractors, and answer leakage are addressed.
- Answer-critical diagrams, graphs, tables, labels, alt text, and exact layers are sufficient.

## Curriculum, Language, Rights

- Track, grade, topic, and standard alignment are supported within the stated scope.
- English/Chinese language variants are natural, semantically equivalent where required, and age appropriate.
- Source distance, licensing, attribution, privacy, and private-corpus boundaries are acceptable.
- Reports contain no protected question text, answer keys, raw provider responses, credentials, or raw protected row/item/student/private-corpus identifiers. Exact candidate/package identity and hash-bound evidence references required for currentness remain permitted.

## Decision Artifact

- Checks run/not run, review method, scope counts, issue counts, blockers, assumptions, and residual risks are recorded.
- Verdict is one of the five allowed A18 states.
- Claim ceiling and next owner/action are explicit.
- When the request spans downstream stages, conditional routes remain distinct even for a blocked A18 decision: Manifest/Shadow/Receipt/currentness goes to Promotion after content acceptance; production/release/deployment/live work goes to Release only after a current Promotion handoff.
- Favorable content QA does not claim Promotion, CI, deployment, or live behavior.
