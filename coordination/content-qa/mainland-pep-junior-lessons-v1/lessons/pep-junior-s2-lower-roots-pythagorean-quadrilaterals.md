## 简体中文教材

### 引入
根式让长度保持精确，勾股定理让直角关系可以计算，也可以反过来判断。

### 学习目标
1. 化简简单二次根式。
2. 正确使用勾股定理及其逆定理。
3. 根据性质判断平行四边形和特殊四边形。

### 预备热身
判断 \(3^2+4^2\) 是否等于 \(5^2\)，并说明这能提示什么几何关系。

### 核心讲解
二次根式化简时，把被开方数分解出完全平方因数。勾股定理只适用于直角三角形：\(a^2+b^2=c^2\)。逆定理可以用边长关系判断一个三角形是否为直角三角形。平行四边形的对边平行且相等，对角相等；矩形、菱形和正方形还具有额外性质。

### 例题精讲
例1 例1：根式化简
化简 \(\sqrt{72}\)。
\(72=36\times2\)，所以 \(\sqrt{72}=\sqrt{36}\sqrt2=6\sqrt2\)。
检查: \((6\sqrt2)^2=36\times2=72\)，与原被开方数一致。

例2 例2：判断直角
三角形三边长为 \(6\)、\(8\)、\(10\)。它是直角三角形吗？
最长边为 \(10\)。因为 \(6^2+8^2=36+64=100\)，且 \(10^2=100\)，所以满足勾股定理逆定理，是直角三角形。
检查: 必须把最长边作为 \(c\) 检查。

### 常见错误
1. 把二次根式随意拆成加法。
2. 没有确认直角就使用勾股定理。
3. 把平行四边形、矩形、菱形性质混用。

### 误区诊断
1. 若学生写 \(\sqrt{25+9}=5+3\)，请用 \(\sqrt{34}\) 与 \(8\) 比较验证。
2. 若学生说所有平行四边形对角线相等，请让他画一个普通平行四边形反例。

### 策略清单
1. 根式先找完全平方因数。
2. 勾股定理先确认直角或最长边。
3. 逆定理要比较两个平方和。
4. 四边形判断要说出足够条件。

### 小练习
小练习 1: 化简 \(\sqrt{50}\)。
答案: 5sqrt2. \(50=25\times2\)，所以 \(\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt2\)。
小练习 2: 直角三角形两直角边为 \(5\) 和 \(12\)，斜边是多少？
答案: 13. \(5^2+12^2=25+144=169\)，所以斜边为 \(13\)。
小练习 3: 平行四边形一组对边长为 \(9\)，对应对边长是多少？
答案: 9. 平行四边形对边相等，所以对应对边为 \(9\)。

### 考试策略
含长度的几何题先判断类型和条件，能保留根式时不要过早取近似值。

### 拓展任务
设计一个坐标中的四边形，用边长或斜率说明它是否为平行四边形。

### 出门票
为什么三边 \(5,6,7\) 不能直接套用 \(a^2+b^2=c^2\) 求新边？

### 术语表
二次根式: 形如根号下非负数的式子。
勾股定理: 直角三角形两直角边平方和等于斜边平方。
逆定理: 把原定理的条件和结论反过来形成的判断。
平行四边形: 两组对边分别平行的四边形。

## English Lesson Textbook

### Hook
Radicals keep lengths exact, and the Pythagorean theorem lets us calculate right-triangle lengths and also test whether a triangle is right.

### Objectives
1. Simplify simple square radicals.
2. Use the Pythagorean theorem and its converse correctly.
3. Classify parallelograms and special quadrilaterals by properties.

### Prerequisite Warm-Up
Check whether \(3^2+4^2\) equals \(5^2\) and explain the geometric relationship this suggests.

### Core Concept
To simplify a square radical, factor out perfect-square factors. The Pythagorean theorem applies only to right triangles: \(a^2+b^2=c^2\). Its converse can test whether a triangle is right. In a parallelogram, opposite sides are parallel and equal, and opposite angles are equal. Rectangles, rhombi, and squares have additional properties.

### Worked Examples
Example 1 Example 1: Simplify a Radical
Simplify \(\sqrt{72}\).
\(72=36\times2\), so \(\sqrt{72}=\sqrt{36}\sqrt2=6\sqrt2\).
Check: \((6\sqrt2)^2=36\times2=72\), matching the original radicand.

Example 2 Example 2: Test for a Right Triangle
A triangle has side lengths \(6\), \(8\), and \(10\). Is it a right triangle?
The longest side is \(10\). Since \(6^2+8^2=36+64=100\) and \(10^2=100\), the converse of the Pythagorean theorem applies, so it is a right triangle.
Check: The longest side must be used as \(c\) in the check.

### Common Pitfalls
1. Splitting radicals incorrectly over addition.
2. Using the Pythagorean theorem without confirming a right angle.
3. Mixing properties of parallelograms, rectangles, and rhombi.

### Misconception Clinic
1. If a learner writes \(\sqrt{25+9}=5+3\), compare \(\sqrt{34}\) with \(8\).
2. If a learner says all parallelograms have equal diagonals, ask for a non-rectangle parallelogram counterexample.

### Strategy Checklist
1. For radicals, look for perfect-square factors first.
2. For Pythagorean work, confirm a right angle or identify the longest side.
3. For the converse, compare two squared sums.
4. For quadrilateral classification, state sufficient conditions.

### Checkpoints
Checkpoint 1: Simplify \(\sqrt{50}\).
Answer: 5sqrt2. \(50=25\times2\), so \(\sqrt{50}=5\sqrt2\).
Checkpoint 2: A right triangle has legs \(5\) and \(12\). What is the hypotenuse?
Answer: 13. \(5^2+12^2=25+144=169\), so the hypotenuse is \(13\).
Checkpoint 3: In a parallelogram, one side has length \(9\). What is the length of the opposite side?
Answer: 9. Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal, so the opposite side is \(9\).

### Exam-Style Strategy
For geometry tasks with lengths, identify the figure type and conditions first. Keep radicals exact instead of approximating too early.

### Extension
Design a quadrilateral on a coordinate grid and use side lengths or slopes to decide whether it is a parallelogram.

### Exit Ticket
Why can we not directly use \(a^2+b^2=c^2\) to find a new side from sides \(5,6,7\)?

### Glossary
square radical: An expression involving the square root of a nonnegative quantity.
Pythagorean theorem: In a right triangle, the sum of the squares of the legs equals the square of the hypotenuse.
converse: A statement formed by reversing the condition and conclusion of a theorem.
parallelogram: A quadrilateral with both pairs of opposite sides parallel.
