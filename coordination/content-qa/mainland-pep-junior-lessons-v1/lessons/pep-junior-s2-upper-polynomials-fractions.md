## 简体中文教材

### 引入
同一个式子可以展开，也可以分解。会选择方向，运算才会更稳。

### 学习目标
1. 运用乘法公式展开整式。
2. 选择提公因式或公式法进行因式分解。
3. 化简分式并检查限制条件。

### 预备热身
计算 \(6a^2b\div 3ab\)，并说明 \(a\)、\(b\) 不能为什么值。

### 核心讲解
整式乘法常用分配律和公式，如 \((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\)。因式分解是展开的逆过程，目标是把多项式写成乘积。分式化简前要先写出分母不为 \(0\) 的限制，分式方程求解后还要检验是否产生增根。

### 例题精讲
例1 例1：公式展开
展开 \((x-4)^2\)。
用公式 \((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\)，得 \((x-4)^2=x^2-8x+16\)。
检查: 中间项是 \(-2\times x\times4=-8x\)，不能漏掉。

例2 例2：分式化简
化简 \(\frac{x^2-9}{x^2+3x}\)。
先分解：\(x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)\)，\(x^2+3x=x(x+3)\)。当 \(x\ne0\) 且 \(x\ne-3\) 时，可约去 \(x+3\)，得 \(\frac{x-3}{x}\)。
检查: 被约去的因式对应限制条件，不能把限制也约掉。

### 常见错误
1. 误用乘法公式，把所有二项式平方都写成两项。
2. 提公因式时没有提完整。
3. 分式约分后忘记分母限制。

### 误区诊断
1. 若学生写 \((x+5)^2=x^2+25\)，请让他用 \((x+5)(x+5)\) 展开验证。
2. 若学生把 \(\frac{x+2}{x}\) 约成 \(2\)，请强调只能约乘法因式，不能约加法项。

### 策略清单
1. 先观察是否有公因式。
2. 再判断是否符合平方差或完全平方公式。
3. 分式先写限制再约分。
4. 分式方程最后代回原分母检查。

### 小练习
小练习 1: 分解 \(6x^2+9x\)。
答案: 3x(2x+3). 公因式为 \(3x\)，所以 \(6x^2+9x=3x(2x+3)\)。
小练习 2: 展开 \((2a+3)(2a-3)\)。
答案: 4a^2-9. 平方差公式：\((2a)^2-3^2=4a^2-9\)。
小练习 3: 分式 \(\frac{5}{x-1}\) 中 \(x\) 不能等于多少？
答案: 1. 分母不能为 \(0\)，所以 \(x-1\ne0\)，\(x\ne1\)。

### 考试策略
符号运算题先看结构再动手，分式题先写限制；若出现约分，检查被约因式是否带来禁值。

### 拓展任务
编写一个分式化简题，要求分子含平方差、分母含公因式，并写出限制条件。

### 出门票
为什么 \((x-4)^2\) 的展开式有中间项？

### 术语表
整式乘法: 把多项式相乘并合并同类项。
因式分解: 把多项式写成几个因式乘积。
公因式: 各项共同含有的因式。
分式: 分母中含有字母的式子。

## English Lesson Textbook

### Hook
The same expression can be expanded or factored. Choosing the direction makes symbolic work steadier.

### Objectives
1. Use product formulas to expand polynomials.
2. Choose common-factor or formula methods for factorization.
3. Simplify algebraic fractions and check restrictions.

### Prerequisite Warm-Up
Compute \(6a^2b\div 3ab\) and state which values \(a\) and \(b\) cannot take.

### Core Concept
Polynomial multiplication often uses the distributive property and formulas such as \((a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2\). Factorization reverses expansion by writing a polynomial as a product. Before simplifying an algebraic fraction, record restrictions that denominators cannot be \(0\). After solving a fraction equation, check for extraneous solutions.

### Worked Examples
Example 1 Example 1: Formula Expansion
Expand \((x-4)^2\).
Use \((a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2\) to get \((x-4)^2=x^2-8x+16\).
Check: The middle term is \(-2\times x\times4=-8x\); it must not be omitted.

Example 2 Example 2: Simplify an Algebraic Fraction
Simplify \(\frac{x^2-9}{x^2+3x}\).
Factor first: \(x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)\) and \(x^2+3x=x(x+3)\). When \(x\ne0\) and \(x\ne-3\), cancel \(x+3\) to get \(\frac{x-3}{x}\).
Check: A canceled factor still creates a restriction; the restriction cannot be canceled away.

### Common Pitfalls
1. Misusing product formulas and writing every binomial square as two terms.
2. Failing to factor out the complete common factor.
3. Forgetting denominator restrictions after simplifying a fraction.

### Misconception Clinic
1. If a learner writes \((x+5)^2=x^2+25\), ask them to expand \((x+5)(x+5)\).
2. If a learner changes \(\frac{x+2}{x}\) to \(2\), emphasize that only factors, not addends, can be canceled.

### Strategy Checklist
1. First look for a common factor.
2. Then check for difference-of-squares or perfect-square patterns.
3. For fractions, write restrictions before canceling.
4. For fraction equations, substitute back into original denominators.

### Checkpoints
Checkpoint 1: Factor \(6x^2+9x\).
Answer: 3x(2x+3). The common factor is \(3x\), so \(6x^2+9x=3x(2x+3)\).
Checkpoint 2: Expand \((2a+3)(2a-3)\).
Answer: 4a^2-9. Difference of squares: \((2a)^2-3^2=4a^2-9\).
Checkpoint 3: In \(\frac{5}{x-1}\), what value can \(x\) not equal?
Answer: 1. The denominator cannot be \(0\), so \(x-1\ne0\) and \(x\ne1\).

### Exam-Style Strategy
For symbolic tasks, inspect structure before operating. For fractions, write restrictions first; if canceling occurs, check whether the canceled factor creates forbidden values.

### Extension
Create a simplification problem where the numerator has a difference of squares and the denominator has a common factor, then state the restrictions.

### Exit Ticket
Why does the expansion of \((x-4)^2\) have a middle term?

### Glossary
polynomial multiplication: Multiplying polynomials and combining like terms.
factorization: Writing a polynomial as a product of factors.
common factor: A factor shared by all terms.
algebraic fraction: An expression with a variable in the denominator.
