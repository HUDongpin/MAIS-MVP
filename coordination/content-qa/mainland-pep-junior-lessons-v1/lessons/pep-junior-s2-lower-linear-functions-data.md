## 简体中文教材

### 引入
一次函数描述稳定变化，数据统计帮助我们选择最合适的代表值。

### 学习目标
1. 理解一次函数的变化率和截距。
2. 在表格、图象和表达式之间转换。
3. 根据问题选择平均数、中位数或众数。

### 预备热身
数对 \((0,3)\) 和 \((2,7)\) 的纵坐标每当横坐标增加 \(1\) 时变化多少？

### 核心讲解
一次函数常写成 \(y=kx+b\)。其中 \(k\) 表示每增加 \(1\) 个横坐标单位时纵坐标的变化量，\(b\) 是图象与 \(y\) 轴的交点纵坐标。数据分析中，平均数受极端值影响，中位数强调中间位置，众数强调出现次数最多的值。

### 例题精讲
例1 例1：求一次函数表达式
一次函数经过 \((0,4)\) 和 \((3,10)\)。求表达式。
因为 \(x=0\) 时 \(y=4\)，所以 \(b=4\)。斜率 \(k=\frac{10-4}{3-0}=2\)，所以 \(y=2x+4\)。
检查: 代入 \(x=3\) 得 \(y=10\)，符合第二个点。

例2 例2：选择统计量
一组练习用时为 \(8,9,9,10,24\) 分钟。若想描述大多数同学的典型用时，平均数和中位数哪个更合适？
平均数为 \(12\)，被 \(24\) 拉高。中位数为 \(9\)，更接近大多数数据，所以中位数更合适。
检查: 选择统计量要看问题目的，而不是只算一个数。

### 常见错误
1. 把斜率和截距的意义混淆。
2. 点没有代入图象表达式检查。
3. 不看情境就固定使用平均数。

### 误区诊断
1. 若学生认为 \(b\) 是横截距，请让他代入 \(x=0\) 观察 \(y\)。
2. 若数据有极端值，请让学生比较平均数和中位数的变化。

### 策略清单
1. 找一次函数先求变化率。
2. 用已知点确认截距或常数项。
3. 图象、表格、式子要互相检查。
4. 统计量选择要说明原因。

### 小练习
小练习 1: 函数 \(y=3x-2\) 的斜率是多少？
答案: 3. \(y=kx+b\) 中 \(k=3\)。
小练习 2: 点 \((2,5)\) 是否在 \(y=2x+1\) 上？
答案: yes. 代入 \(x=2\) 得 \(2\times2+1=5\)，成立。
小练习 3: 数据 \(4,4,6,7,9\) 的众数是多少？
答案: 4. \(4\) 出现次数最多。

### 考试策略
函数题用点代回，数据题用一句话说明统计量为什么适合这个问题。

### 拓展任务
记录五天学习时间，分别计算平均数和中位数，并说明哪个更能代表这一周。

### 出门票
在 \(y=kx+b\) 中，\(k\) 和 \(b\) 各表示什么？

### 术语表
一次函数: 图象为直线且变化率固定的函数。
斜率: 横坐标增加一个单位时纵坐标的变化量。
截距: 图象与坐标轴的交点对应数值。
中位数: 按顺序排列后位于中间的数据。

## English Lesson Textbook

### Hook
Linear functions describe steady change, and data statistics help us choose an appropriate representative value.

### Objectives
1. Understand rate of change and intercept in a linear function.
2. Move among tables, graphs, and formulas.
3. Choose mean, median, or mode according to the question.

### Prerequisite Warm-Up
For points \((0,3)\) and \((2,7)\), how much does the y-coordinate change when the x-coordinate increases by \(1\)?

### Core Concept
A linear function is often written as \(y=kx+b\). Here \(k\) is the change in y when x increases by \(1\), and \(b\) is the y-coordinate of the y-intercept. In data analysis, the mean is affected by outliers, the median emphasizes middle position, and the mode emphasizes the most frequent value.

### Worked Examples
Example 1 Example 1: Find a Linear Formula
A linear function passes through \((0,4)\) and \((3,10)\). Find its formula.
Since \(y=4\) when \(x=0\), \(b=4\). The slope is \(k=\frac{10-4}{3-0}=2\), so \(y=2x+4\).
Check: Substitute \(x=3\) to get \(y=10\), matching the second point.

Example 2 Example 2: Choose a Statistic
Practice times are \(8,9,9,10,24\) minutes. To describe a typical time for most students, which is better: mean or median?
The mean is \(12\), pulled upward by \(24\). The median is \(9\), closer to most data values, so the median is more suitable.
Check: Choosing a statistic depends on the purpose, not just computing one number.

### Common Pitfalls
1. Confusing the meanings of slope and intercept.
2. Not checking whether a point satisfies the formula.
3. Always using the mean without considering context.

### Misconception Clinic
1. If a learner thinks \(b\) is the x-intercept, ask them to substitute \(x=0\) and observe \(y\).
2. When data include an outlier, ask learners to compare changes in mean and median.

### Strategy Checklist
1. Find the rate of change first for a linear function.
2. Use a known point to confirm the intercept or constant term.
3. Check graphs, tables, and formulas against each other.
4. Explain the reason for choosing a statistic.

### Checkpoints
Checkpoint 1: What is the slope of \(y=3x-2\)?
Answer: 3. In \(y=kx+b\), \(k=3\).
Checkpoint 2: Does point \((2,5)\) lie on \(y=2x+1\)?
Answer: yes. Substitute \(x=2\) to get \(2\times2+1=5\), so yes.
Checkpoint 3: What is the mode of \(4,4,6,7,9\)?
Answer: 4. \(4\) appears most often.

### Exam-Style Strategy
For function items, substitute points back. For data items, write one sentence explaining why the statistic fits the question.

### Extension
Record five days of study time, compute the mean and median, and explain which better represents the week.

### Exit Ticket
In \(y=kx+b\), what do \(k\) and \(b\) represent?

### Glossary
linear function: A function with a straight-line graph and constant rate of change.
slope: The change in y for a one-unit increase in x.
intercept: The value where a graph crosses an axis.
median: The middle value when data are ordered.
