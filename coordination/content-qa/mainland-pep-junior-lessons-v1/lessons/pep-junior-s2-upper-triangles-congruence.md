## 简体中文教材

### 引入
几何证明的关键不是“看起来一样”，而是找到足够的条件说明它们必然一样。

### 学习目标
1. 运用三角形边角关系分析图形。
2. 识别并使用全等判定条件。
3. 用轴对称性质解决角、边和位置问题。

### 预备热身
三角形三个内角和是多少？若两个角为 \(48^\circ\) 和 \(67^\circ\)，第三个角是多少？

### 核心讲解
三角形内角和为 \(180^\circ\)。证明两个三角形全等时，要先对应顶点，再选用合适条件，如 \(SSS\)、\(SAS\)、\(ASA\) 或直角三角形中的斜边直角边条件。轴对称图形中，对称点到对称轴距离相等，对应线段和对应角相等。

### 例题精讲
例1 例1：三角形角度
三角形 \(ABC\) 中，\(\angle A=52^\circ\)，\(\angle B=61^\circ\)，求 \(\angle C\)。
\(\angle C=180^\circ-52^\circ-61^\circ=67^\circ\)。
检查: 三个角相加为 \(52^\circ+61^\circ+67^\circ=180^\circ\)。

例2 例2：全等证明思路
已知 \(AB=DE\)，\(AC=DF\)，\(\angle A=\angle D\)。说明 \(\triangle ABC\) 与 \(\triangle DEF\) 为什么全等。
两组对应边相等，且夹角相等。对应顺序为 \(A\leftrightarrow D\)，\(B\leftrightarrow E\)，\(C\leftrightarrow F\)，所以由 \(SAS\) 得两三角形全等。
检查: 使用 \(SAS\) 时，角必须是两条已知边的夹角。

### 常见错误
1. 对应顶点没有匹配就写全等。
2. 把不完整条件当作全等判定。
3. 只凭图形像轴对称就下结论。

### 误区诊断
1. 若学生用 \(SSA\) 判定全等，请给出一个反例草图说明条件不够。
2. 若学生找错对应边，请让他按顶点顺序重新读三角形名称。

### 策略清单
1. 先写对应顶点。
2. 标出已知边和角。
3. 判断条件是否满足某个全等判定。
4. 证明后再使用对应边角相等。

### 小练习
小练习 1: 三角形两角为 \(40^\circ\) 和 \(75^\circ\)，第三角是多少？
答案: 65 degrees. \(180^\circ-40^\circ-75^\circ=65^\circ\)。
小练习 2: \(AB=PQ\)，\(BC=QR\)，\(AC=PR\) 可用什么判定全等？
答案: SSS. 三组对应边相等，所以用 \(SSS\)。
小练习 3: 轴对称中，对称点到对称轴的距离有什么关系？
答案: equal. 对称点到对称轴距离相等。

### 考试策略
证明题先列“已知、要证、对应”，每一步只使用已经给出或已经证明的关系。

### 拓展任务
画一个轴对称图形，标出两组对应点，并写出两条可以证明的边角关系。

### 出门票
为什么 \(SAS\) 中的角必须是两条已知边的夹角？

### 术语表
全等: 形状和大小完全相同。
对应顶点: 两个图形中位置相互匹配的顶点。
轴对称: 沿一条直线折叠后两部分重合。
判定条件: 足以推出结论的一组条件。

## English Lesson Textbook

### Hook
The key in geometric proof is not that figures look the same, but that enough conditions force them to be the same.

### Objectives
1. Use triangle side-angle relationships to analyze figures.
2. Identify and apply triangle congruence criteria.
3. Use symmetry properties to solve angle, side, and position problems.

### Prerequisite Warm-Up
What is the sum of a triangle's interior angles? If two angles are \(48^\circ\) and \(67^\circ\), what is the third angle?

### Core Concept
The interior angles of a triangle sum to \(180^\circ\). To prove two triangles congruent, first match corresponding vertices, then choose a criterion such as \(SSS\), \(SAS\), \(ASA\), or the hypotenuse-leg condition for right triangles. In axial symmetry, corresponding points are the same distance from the axis, and corresponding segments and angles are equal.

### Worked Examples
Example 1 Example 1: Triangle Angles
In triangle \(ABC\), \(\angle A=52^\circ\) and \(\angle B=61^\circ\). Find \(\angle C\).
\(\angle C=180^\circ-52^\circ-61^\circ=67^\circ\).
Check: The three angles sum to \(52^\circ+61^\circ+67^\circ=180^\circ\).

Example 2 Example 2: Congruence Reasoning
Given \(AB=DE\), \(AC=DF\), and \(\angle A=\angle D\), explain why \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle DEF\) are congruent.
Two pairs of corresponding sides are equal, and the included angle is equal. The correspondence is \(A\leftrightarrow D\), \(B\leftrightarrow E\), \(C\leftrightarrow F\), so the triangles are congruent by \(SAS\).
Check: When using \(SAS\), the angle must be included between the two known sides.

### Common Pitfalls
1. Writing congruence before matching corresponding vertices.
2. Treating incomplete information as a congruence criterion.
3. Concluding symmetry from appearance only.

### Misconception Clinic
1. If a learner uses \(SSA\) as a congruence test, show a counterexample sketch to demonstrate insufficiency.
2. If a learner mismatches corresponding sides, ask them to reread the triangle names in vertex order.

### Strategy Checklist
1. Write corresponding vertices first.
2. Mark known sides and angles.
3. Decide whether the information fits a congruence criterion.
4. After proof, use equality of corresponding parts.

### Checkpoints
Checkpoint 1: A triangle has angles \(40^\circ\) and \(75^\circ\). What is the third angle?
Answer: 65 degrees. \(180^\circ-40^\circ-75^\circ=65^\circ\).
Checkpoint 2: If \(AB=PQ\), \(BC=QR\), and \(AC=PR\), which criterion proves congruence?
Answer: SSS. Three pairs of corresponding sides are equal, so use \(SSS\).
Checkpoint 3: In axial symmetry, what is true about the distances from corresponding points to the axis?
Answer: equal. Corresponding points are the same distance from the axis.

### Exam-Style Strategy
For proofs, list given facts, target statement, and correspondence first. Each step should use only a given or already-proven relationship.

### Extension
Draw an axially symmetric figure, mark two pairs of corresponding points, and write two side-angle relationships that can be proven.

### Exit Ticket
Why must the angle in \(SAS\) be the included angle between the two known sides?

### Glossary
congruence: Exactly the same shape and size.
corresponding vertices: Matching vertices in two figures.
axial symmetry: A figure matches after folding along a line.
criterion: A set of conditions sufficient to prove a conclusion.
