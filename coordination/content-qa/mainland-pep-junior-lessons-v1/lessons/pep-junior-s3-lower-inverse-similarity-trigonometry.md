## 简体中文教材

### 引入
当图形变大变小、距离难以直接测量时，比例和三角函数可以把问题转化为可计算关系。

### 学习目标
1. 解释反比例函数图象和常数的意义。
2. 使用相似判定并找准对应边。
3. 选择正弦、余弦或正切解决直角三角形问题。

### 预备热身
若两个相似三角形的相似比为 \(2:5\)，较小的三角形一边为 \(6\)，对应较大的三角形边长是多少？

### 核心讲解
反比例函数常写成 \(y=\frac{k}{x}\)，其中 \(xy=k\) 表示乘积保持不变。相似图形对应角相等、对应边成比例。直角三角形中，\(\sin A\)、\(\cos A\)、\(\tan A\) 分别连接角与对边、邻边、斜边之间的比例。视图问题要先确认观察方向。

### 例题精讲
例1 例1：反比例函数
反比例函数 \(y=\frac{k}{x}\) 经过点 \((3,8)\)。求 \(k\)，并求当 \(x=6\) 时的 \(y\)。
由 \(xy=k\) 得 \(k=3\times8=24\)。当 \(x=6\) 时，\(y=\frac{24}{6}=4\)。
检查: 两个点都满足乘积 \(xy=24\)。

例2 例2：直角三角函数
直角三角形中，角 \(A\) 的对边为 \(9\)，邻边为 \(12\)。求 \(\tan A\)。
\(\tan A=\frac{\text{对边}}{\text{邻边}}=\frac{9}{12}=\frac34\)。
检查: 正切只用对边和邻边，不用斜边。

### 常见错误
1. 把反比例函数的象限读错。
2. 相似比顺序写反。
3. 三角函数中对边、邻边选错。
4. 三视图观察方向混淆。

### 误区诊断
1. 若学生把 \(y=\frac{12}{x}\) 当作一次函数，请比较 \(x\) 加倍时 \(y\) 是否也加倍。
2. 若学生写相似比 \(5:2\)，请让他先标“小:大”或“大:小”。

### 策略清单
1. 反比例函数先找乘积常数。
2. 相似题先标对应顶点和对应边。
3. 三角函数先确定角，再找对边、邻边、斜边。
4. 视图题先写观察方向。

### 小练习
小练习 1: \(y=\frac{18}{x}\) 中，当 \(x=3\) 时 \(y\) 是多少？
答案: 6. \(y=\frac{18}{3}=6\)。
小练习 2: 两个相似三角形边长比为 \(3:4\)，较小的三角形一边 \(15\)，对应较大的边是多少？
答案: 20. 比例放大倍数为 \(\frac43\)，所以 \(15\times\frac43=20\)。
小练习 3: 若直角三角形中 \(\sin A=\frac{5}{13}\)，对边与斜边的比是多少？
答案: 5:13. \(\sin A\) 就是对边与斜边之比，所以为 \(5:13\)。

### 考试策略
测量应用题先画比例关系图，再决定用相似、反比例还是三角函数；不要先代公式再找边。

### 拓展任务
设计一个测量树高的问题，给出角度或相似影长数据，并写出完整比例关系。

### 出门票
使用 \(\tan A\) 前，为什么必须先确定角 \(A\)？

### 术语表
反比例函数: 形如 y 等于常数除以 x 的函数。
相似: 形状相同、大小可以不同的图形关系。
正切: 直角三角形内某个锐角的对边与邻边之比。
视图: 从某个方向观察立体得到的平面图。

## English Lesson Textbook

### Hook
When figures scale or distances are hard to measure directly, ratios and trigonometric functions turn the problem into a computable relationship.

### Objectives
1. Explain the graph and constant in an inverse proportional function.
2. Use similarity criteria and identify corresponding sides accurately.
3. Choose sine, cosine, or tangent to solve right-triangle problems.

### Prerequisite Warm-Up
If two similar triangles have scale ratio \(2:5\) and a side of the smaller triangle is \(6\), what is the corresponding side of the larger triangle?

### Core Concept
An inverse proportional function is often written as \(y=\frac{k}{x}\), where \(xy=k\) means the product stays constant. Similar figures have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides. In a right triangle, \(\sin A\), \(\cos A\), and \(\tan A\) connect an angle with ratios involving opposite side, adjacent side, and hypotenuse. For view problems, confirm the viewing direction first.

### Worked Examples
Example 1 Example 1: Inverse Proportion
The inverse proportional function \(y=\frac{k}{x}\) passes through \((3,8)\). Find \(k\), and find \(y\) when \(x=6\).
Since \(xy=k\), \(k=3\times8=24\). When \(x=6\), \(y=\frac{24}{6}=4\).
Check: Both points satisfy product \(xy=24\).

Example 2 Example 2: Right-Triangle Trigonometry
In a right triangle, the side opposite angle \(A\) is \(9\) and the adjacent side is \(12\). Find \(\tan A\).
\(\tan A=\frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}=\frac{9}{12}=\frac34\).
Check: Tangent uses opposite and adjacent sides, not the hypotenuse.

### Common Pitfalls
1. Misreading quadrants of an inverse proportional graph.
2. Reversing the order of a similarity ratio.
3. Choosing the wrong opposite or adjacent side in trigonometry.
4. Confusing the viewing direction in projection views.

### Misconception Clinic
1. If a learner treats \(y=\frac{12}{x}\) as linear, compare what happens to \(y\) when \(x\) doubles.
2. If a learner writes similarity ratio \(5:2\), ask them to label "small:large" or "large:small" first.

### Strategy Checklist
1. For inverse proportion, find the constant product first.
2. For similarity, mark corresponding vertices and sides.
3. For trigonometry, identify the angle before opposite, adjacent, and hypotenuse.
4. For projection views, write the viewing direction first.

### Checkpoints
Checkpoint 1: In \(y=\frac{18}{x}\), what is \(y\) when \(x=3\)?
Answer: 6. \(y=\frac{18}{3}=6\).
Checkpoint 2: Two similar triangles have side ratio \(3:4\). A side of the smaller triangle is \(15\); what is the corresponding larger side?
Answer: 20. The scale factor is \(\frac43\), so \(15\times\frac43=20\).
Checkpoint 3: If \(\sin A=\frac{5}{13}\) in a right triangle, what is the ratio of opposite side to hypotenuse?
Answer: 5:13. \(\sin A\) is the ratio of opposite side to hypotenuse, so it is \(5:13\).

### Exam-Style Strategy
For measurement applications, draw the ratio relationship first, then decide whether similarity, inverse proportion, or trigonometry applies. Do not substitute into a formula before identifying sides.

### Extension
Design a tree-height measurement problem using an angle or similar-shadow data, then write the full ratio relationship.

### Exit Ticket
Before using \(\tan A\), why must angle \(A\) be identified first?

### Glossary
inverse proportional function: A function where y equals a constant divided by x.
similarity: A relationship where figures have the same shape but may differ in size.
tangent: For an acute angle in a right triangle, the ratio of opposite side to adjacent side.
projection view: A plane view of a solid from a chosen direction.
