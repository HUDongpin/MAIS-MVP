### What this means for the remediation plan

**The grader item is untouched and should stay the top blocker — but rewrite its justification.** The audit's marquee number (31.3% on Chinese units) is bad arithmetic and should be dropped from the plan; the real figure is that a mainland learner who types the unit their own textbook taught them is rejected on **7,301 of 7,307** numeric items, and one who writes the standard 「答：…。」 sentence is rejected on **all 7,307**. That is a bigger, simpler, more defensible case than the one the plan currently makes. Three sub-items should be re-scoped rather than fixed as written: the `²` line is a **no-op** (NFKC already collapses it) and its live short-key exposure is 15 items; `π` is down to 12 items; the `、`-ordering hazard has **zero** live instances. The load-bearing fixes are, in order: Chinese unit stripping (7,307), ideographic 。 (168 — note the 2026-08-25 ASCII-period fix does *not* cover U+3002), `√`/`sqrt` (188), and `或` set-comparison (33). Implement the unit rule in `questionAnswerMatches`, gated on `question.answer` carrying no unit — the audit's own correction is right, and **548 items** whose key *is* number+unit prove why a symmetric strip in `answerMatches` would be a regression.

**Plan item 7 (PEP `practiceQuestionIds`) needs no re-scoping at all** — 57/57, 160–320 vs 8, 20.0×–40.0× all reproduce to the digit, and the two `userStore.ts` call sites are unchanged apart from line numbers (2313→2420, 7330→13091). It should probably move ahead of the content campaigns, since it is a 57-entry data edit against a corpus that is not being re-cut.

**The lesson-language work has collapsed to a quarter of its original size and should be re-planned around that.** "222 of 335" is dead as a headline: 196 of those lessons no longer ship and the pep-junior 2 do not reproduce, leaving **24 bnu-high lessons / 188 occurrences** — one generator, one `重点关注`/`围绕` clause. Same for the boilerplate item: 221 → 24, same 24 lessons. But note the withdrawal fixed nothing at source — `mainlandBnuPrimaryLessons.ts:84`, `mainlandHjbPrimaryLessons.ts:63`, `mainlandHjbHighLessons.ts:69` still interpolate raw slug arrays into zh-Hans, so **any decision to re-promote those packs re-opens all 196 lessons**; the slug fix should be treated as a precondition of re-promotion, not as closed. The one lesson finding that is genuinely unchanged and genuinely blocking is the **hjb-junior mad-libs pack**: 22 lessons, one skeleton, still `productionReady: true`, still shipping to learners behind 1,500 questions, and it is the only China lesson defect the withdrawal did not touch.
