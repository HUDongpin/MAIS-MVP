## Root cause the original audit missed, and it changes the fix

The withdrawn packs' JSON on disk is **clean**: `mainland-hjb-junior-generated-bank-v2-1500` and `mainland-bnu-primary-generated-bank-v2-1500` both contain **0** `term-` tokens and **0** traditional characters in `acceptedAnswers`. The pollution is **injected at build time** by `/Volumes/Starship/MAIS-main-wt/data/hjbQuestionLocalization.ts`:

- line **687** emits the placeholder when a character misses the hand-built dictionary: `` `term-${char.codePointAt(0)?.toString(16) ?? "unknown"}` ``
- lines **754–771** (`localizedHjbGeneratedAcceptedAnswers`) then push `localizedAnswer.en` and `localizedAnswer.zh` straight into `acceptedAnswers`, which is exactly how the machine-English, the `term-XXXX` tokens, and the traditional variants all get into the grading key.

F1 + F2 + F3 in `acceptedAnswers` are therefore **one code defect in one file**, not a 2,817-item data migration.

## What this means for the remediation plan

Half the plan's tonnage evaporated, but nothing was *fixed* — it was unplugged. `data/mainlandBnuPrimaryQuestions.ts`, `mainlandHjbPrimaryQuestions.ts` and `mainlandHjbHighQuestions.ts` are all still on disk, still imported by `lib/questionBankSolvability.ts`, and one import line in `data/questions.ts` away from returning; since their pollution is generated by the shared localization function rather than baked into their packs, **re-promoting any of them reinstates the full original numbers**. So treat the term-/Hant/machine-English residue as MOOT-pending, not closed.

Re-prioritise as follows. Item 5 of the plan ("strip pipeline text") should be split: the **3,972 prompt labels and 2,515 explanation tails are completely untouched** — same items, same counts, same ids, and they are the single largest block of learner-visible garbage left, so they stay at the top and their scope estimate needs no revision. The **1,277 hjb-junior items with `term-XXXX` in `prompt.en`/`explanation.en`/`options[].en`** are the only *client-rendered* instance of the placeholder class and are worth more than the 415 `acceptedAnswers` hits, which never reach the browser. All of it is fixed by repairing `hjbQuestionLocalization.ts:687` and `:754-771` plus a re-derive — a far smaller job than the plan assumes, and one that also inoculates the three withdrawn packs.

Two claims should be struck. The pep-high "3,021 / 62.9%" figure is a mis-citation of a different finding and is in any case an undercount — the honest number is 3,900 / 81.3%. And "70 items tell the learner the displayed answer is wrong" is dead: the audit's own predicate yields 54, and in all 115 cases the stored answer already matches the memo's corrected result. The 115 memos remain a blocker because reviewer scratch prose is rendered as 解析 and 28 of them cut off mid-sentence — but the "we ship known-wrong answer keys" framing that justified the blocker severity is not supported by current main and should not be repeated to stakeholders.
