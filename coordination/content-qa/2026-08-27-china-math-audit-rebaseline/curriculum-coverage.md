## What this means for the remediation plan

The coverage half of this audit is essentially **fully intact and the plan should not be discounted**. Six of the eight claims survive unchanged, and two of them (尺规作图, 科学记数法) are broader than the audit stated — they are zero across all 11,700 shipped items, not just PEP. Every PEP-scoped finding survives with its *original denominator*, because pep-primary/junior/high were never touched: the 7,200-item PEP bank on main is byte-identical to what the audit read. Any remediation task scoped to PEP can be lifted from the plan verbatim, including the specific ids — `pep-junior-s3-upper-quadratics-circle-probability` and `pep-high-s4-quadratic-inequalities` both still exist and still contain exactly the defects described.

Only one item is genuinely dead as shipped work: **hjb-high**. Mark those tasks MOOT, not FIXED — the pack is still in the tree, the numbers reproduce on it today, and S5/S6 are *more* degenerate than the audit reported (500/500 in single-skeleton topics at both grades). Any decision to re-promote hjb-high re-imports the finding whole, so the remediation plan's hjb-high section should be retained as a **gate condition on re-promotion** rather than deleted.

Two things the plan should now say that it could not say on 2026-08-19. First, the **spine got worse, not better**: 335 → 138 topics, and the withdrawal took out bnu-primary (97 topics) and hjb-primary (70 topics) — the two banks the audit's own §4 named as the good ones. The remediation target of ~151 PEP topics is now not just a quality goal but the only granularity work left, since the granular publishers are no longer shipping. Second, **综合与实践 is now zero across the entire China corpus**, not just PEP: the withdrawal removed the 278 BNU 数学好玩 items and ~102 HJB 数学广场 items that were the product's only real instances of one of the four 课标 领域. That escalates the 综合与实践 task from "PEP gap" to "product-wide gap" and it should be re-prioritised accordingly.

Finally, a governance note the plan should absorb: the withdrawal is *subtractive only*. Nothing was authored, fixed, or re-reviewed in five weeks. Treating the smaller corpus as progress would be a misread — the defect density per shipped item is unchanged, and the bank lost 6,000 items of which 4,500 were from the two best-rated packs.
