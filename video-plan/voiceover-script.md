Every learner meets mathematics from a different starting point.

MAIS is built for a future where every student has a responsive, bilingual pathway through mathematics.

For teachers, MAIS turns class activity into a daily teaching queue.

Weak topics, repeated mistakes, and at-risk students surface early, before they disappear in averages.

Live checks help teachers decide: continue, pause, or reteach.
