# Dr. Peter Hu Founder Teleprompter

Target duration: 60 seconds.

Tone: calm, credible, human. This should feel like a founder explaining why the product exists, not reading a product brochure.

## Main Take

I am Dr. Peter Hu, developer of MAIS.

I built it to make AI useful inside real learning routines.

The problem is not that students lack effort. Too often, schools lack adaptive infrastructure that notices what each learner needs next.

With Codex, educational research can become working software: dashboards, analytics, visualizations, tutor prompts, and safer iteration.

MAIS is a pilot-ready MVP: bilingual, data-aware, teacher-supportive, and designed for responsible evaluation.

With FII support, MAIS can move from prototype to pilots, helping create prosperous learning environments powered by responsible AI.

## More Personal Alternate Take

I am Dr. Peter Hu, developer of MAIS.

As an educational technology researcher, I care about a simple question: how can AI help real students and real teachers, not just produce impressive demos?

MAIS is my answer.

It gives students a responsive mathematics pathway, with visual models, adaptive practice, and guided AI support.

It gives teachers learning signals they can act on: weak topics, repeated mistakes, at-risk students, and live classroom checks.

With Codex, I can turn research ideas into working software quickly and test them responsibly.

With FII support, MAIS can move from prototype to pilots and help create prosperous learning environments powered by responsible AI.

## Delivery Notes

- Keep sentences short.
- Pause after "MAIS is my answer" if using the alternate take.
- Emphasize "real students and real teachers."
- Avoid saying "we already improved outcomes" unless you have measured evidence.
- Smile lightly on the final sentence.
