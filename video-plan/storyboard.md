## Style Reference From Musa

Borrow the structure, not the content: problem first, simple access point, personalization mechanism, evidence/scale posture, founder/team credibility. For MAIS, the access point is the student/teacher web console, and the evidence posture is current MVP capability plus future pilot ambition.
