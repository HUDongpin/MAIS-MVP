# MAIS FII Innovators Pitch Video Packet

Purpose: produce one English 2:30 master video for FII Innovators Pitch, with three clean chapter exports:

- `00:00-01:00` Student console introduction
- `01:00-01:30` Teacher console introduction
- `01:30-02:30` Dr. Peter Hu founder introduction

Impact posture: vision-led, with clear separation between current MVP capabilities and projected pilot impact. The video should feel ambitious, but no traction, revenue, school adoption, or measured learning-outcome claim should appear unless Dr. Peter Hu supplies evidence before the final edit.

## Artifact Map

- `storyboard.md` - Chinese/English 分镜头脚本 with shot, VO, caption, and production notes.
- `voiceover-script.md` - final English narration and chapter timing.
- `subtitles/mais-fii-master.en.srt` - complete SRT subtitles for the 2:30 master.
- `on-screen-captions.md` - editor-ready title cards and lower thirds.
- `shot-checklist.md` - exact screen and camera shots to capture.
- `recording-checklist.md` - screen-recording, founder-camera, audio, and export checklist.
- `claim-check.md` - claim register: current MVP capability vs future/pilot ambition.
- `codex-production-workflow.md` - how to use Codex to produce, QA, and iterate the video.
- `chapter-markers.csv` - chapter/export timing data.
- `export-guide.md` - exact output files, durations, and QC rules.
- `founder-teleprompter.md` - founder on-camera script and performance notes.
- `fii-application-caption.md` - short application/video caption options.
- `assets/thumbnail-brief.md` - thumbnail direction and optional image-generation prompt.

## Primary FII Alignment

Use the Education track framing: personalized learning pathways, AI-powered tutor support, teacher support, measurable learning signals, feasibility, scalability, and partnership potential.

## Production Rule

The strongest visuals are real MAIS product flows and Dr. Peter Hu speaking directly to camera. Use generated graphics only for title cards, abstract transitions, and thumbnail polish.
